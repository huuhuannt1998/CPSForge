\section*{Ethical Considerations}
All experiments were conducted on an isolated laboratory network containing a single Siemens S7-1200 PLC and a software-based process emulator (Factory~I/O); no production infrastructure or third-party systems were involved. The runtime safety shield enforces mandatory write constraints that prevent the PLC from entering states that could cause equipment damage, even under adversarial input. Attack payloads generated by the LLM are strictly structured and validated before execution; the framework does not produce general-purpose exploit code. We recognise the dual-use potential of automated attack generation tools and have designed CPSForge with safety-by-default behaviour, dry-run modes, and auditable logging to mitigate misuse. The framework is intended solely for defensive research and education.
